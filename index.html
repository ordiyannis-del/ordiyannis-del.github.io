Auto Clips Studio

Auto Clips Studio is a personal desktop tool. It cuts the owner's own long videos into short vertical clips of 90 seconds or less, adds subtitles, writes a caption, and uploads each clip to the owner's TikTok account using the official TikTok API (Login Kit and Content Posting API).

How it works

  1. The user connects her TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and approves access.
  2. The user pastes the link of one of her own videos.
  3. The tool creates vertical clips with subtitles and a caption on her computer.
  4. Each clip is uploaded to her TikTok account, one at a time, with a delay between posts.
